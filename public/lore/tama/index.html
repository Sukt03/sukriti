The Robot Who Learned to Read Moods

A six-panel story about my ICETCI 2026 paper on reading emotions from brain signals in people the model has never met, and the three tricks that helped.

★ Best Presentation Award · IEEE ICETCI 2026 EEG · DEAP · 32 electrodes ~4 min read

The moral of the story

On a test this strict, chasing perfect accuracy misses the point. The better question is which ideas actually help. The two disguises helped the model handle new people, brain-map knowledge (TAMA) helped it tell both moods apart, and words on their own didn't help unless the disguises came with them.
