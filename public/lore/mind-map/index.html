Three Ways to Read a Mind

A six-panel tour of my survey on generative AI for brain decoding: three windows into the brain, the race between sharp and fast, two traps that make scores look better than they are, and a short checklist for doing it honestly.

IEEE ICETCI 2026 Survey · EEG · fMRI · MEG ~4 min read

The moral of the story

Each window into the brain trades something: sharpness, speed, or cost. Generative models have made the pictures and sentences much better, but progress only counts if it holds on new people, new datasets, and without hints.
