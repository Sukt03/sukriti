The Blurry Cat Trial

A six-panel story about my IJCAI–ECAI paper: why the usual scorecards judge brain-decoded images unfairly, and how a jury of AI judges (and then a pocket-sized one) learned to judge them better.

★ Best Poster Award · IJCAI–ECAI 2026 HBAI EEG-to-image · 6,855 image pairs ~4 min read

The moral of the story

For images decoded from the brain, the right question isn't "does it look identical?" but "can you still tell what it was?" Look and meaning need to be judged separately, then together, and a small learned judge can do that cheaply for every new model.
